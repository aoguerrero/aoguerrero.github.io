Enable RDP on Windows Home

  1. Download RDP Wrapper

https://github.com/stascorp/rdpwrap

  1. Execute install.bat

  2. Download updated rdpwrap.ini

https://github.com/sebaxakerhtc/rdpwrap.ini

  1. Stop RDP service
net stop termService

  1. Replace rdpwrap.ini downloaded in step 3 in C:\Program Files\RDP Wrapper

  2. Start RDP service

net start termService
